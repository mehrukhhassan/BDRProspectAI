Company snapshot
Retool (retool.com) — San Francisco–based platform for building internal software, founded by CEO David Hsu (YC W17). Engineering and ops teams use it to assemble internal apps, dashboards, workflows and now AI agents from prebuilt components, on Retool Cloud or self-hosted 

Who it serves: Engineering, data and ops teams at everyone from startups to large enterprises. Publicly referenced customers include Amazon, OpenAI, DoorDash, Plaid, Stripe, Coinbase, Chime, Brex, Pinterest, NBC and Jaguar Land Rover 

. Retool markets "10,000+ companies" and "100M+ hours of work automated" 

 — vendor claim, not independently verified.
Products: Core app builder (rebuilt and relaunched June 2026) 

, Workflows, Agents (enterprise AI agents inheriting existing queries, APIs and integrations as tools) 

, plus an official MCP server so external coding agents like Claude Code can build and deploy into a governed Retool environment 

Positioning in 2026: Fully pivoted to "secure your vibe-coded apps" — AI-assisted building with platform-level governance 

Funding/scale: Famously capital-efficient — ~$165M raised total, last priced around $3.2B (2022 Series C2, Sequoia) 

. Dealroom estimates $100–500M revenue and 100–500 headcount 

 — third-party estimate, treat as soft. Hsu has said publicly the company runs near cash-flow breakeven 

. No 2026 funding round found.
