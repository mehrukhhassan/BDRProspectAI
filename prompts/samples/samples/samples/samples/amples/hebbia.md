Company snapshot
Hebbia (hebbia.com) — New York–based "AI built for the rigor of finance," founded 2020 by George Sivulka (sole founder/CEO, ex-Stanford PhD) 

. Flagship product Matrix is a multi-agent platform that ingests unlimited-length documents and returns structured, cited answers in a spreadsheet-like grid — built for due diligence, deal work and legal review rather than chat 

Who it serves: Asset managers, investment banks, PE firms, law firms and Fortune 500s. Sivulka has said ~30% of all asset managers use it 

; publicly referenced customers include Centerview Partners, Charlesbank, Fenwick 

, and per secondary sources KKR, Morgan Stanley, MetLife, Seyfarth Shaw and the US Air Force 

Scale (estimates, not company-confirmed): Sacra estimates ~$48M ARR as of Aug 2026, up from ~$30M at end-2025 (+76% YoY) 

. Verified: $13M ARR and profitable at the 2024 raise 

Funding: ~$161M raised; $130M Series B at ~$700M (July 2024), led by a16z with Index, GV and Peter Thiel 

. A Presight Shorooq Fund I investment is logged for 14 Jan 2026 

. No new priced round found since 2024 — don't congratulate them on funding.
Company size: Small. Their own careers page lists 19 open roles across NYC and SF 

 — this is a lean, concentrated team, not an enterprise.

Read the hiring mix carefully before you pitch. Hebbia has no SDR or BDR reqs posted. Their customer-facing hiring is "forward deployed" ex-bankers and ex-investors, not volume outbound. A GTM-tech pitch that assumes a large SDR bench will land wrong. The GTM Recruiter req is the honest signal that go-to-market scaling is coming

Chief of Staff to the CEO (your target). At a sole-founder company of this size, a CoS typically owns cross-functional execution and often GTM strategy and planning by default. Likely priorities: making a small GTM team productive, prepping the next raise, and standing up systems ahead of scaling. Realistically they are a router and gatekeeper, sometimes an evaluator, rarely the signer.
