Likely buyer personas (AI / eng / data infra, VP Sales entry point)
Flag on the brief first: a VP of Sales at Gong is a defensible entry point only if your product changes a revenue number. If it's genuinely infrastructure — data pipelines, model serving, observability, eval — the budget almost certainly sits in R&D/Product under co-founder Eilon Reshef 

, not sales. Using the VP Sales as a referral into that org is realistic; expecting them to sign is not. Everything below is hypothesis.
VP of Sales / senior sales leadership (your target). Priorities: sustaining 55%+ growth against a bigger base, landing more $1M+ enterprise deals 

, and IPO-grade forecast accuracy. Angle that works: anything improving rep productivity or forecast reliability. Angle that fails: anything they'd read as "we'll help you do what Gong does."
RevOps / Sales Ops leadership. Gong just shipped Custom Agents specifically so this group can build agents without engineering 

 — they are being handed real build capability and will have opinions on tooling and data plumbing.
R&D / AI engineering leadership (the real infra buyer). Owns the Revenue Graph, model training and the MCP surface 

. This is where an infra sale actually lands.
Simon Frey, CCO (named, verified). New in seat since March 2026 

 — new execs reassess tooling, and the customer org spans 5,000 accounts.
Security / legal / compliance. With a CLO hired in March 

 and likely IPO prep underway, expect heavier diligence than at a typical private company: data residency, model training on customer data, SOC 2, subprocessors.
