Company snapshot
Ramp (ramp.com) — New York-based finance automation platform founded in 2019. It combines corporate cards, expense management, bill pay/AP, procurement, vendor management, travel, treasury/banking and accounting automation in one system, with AI agents handling the manual work 

Who it serves: 70,000+ businesses, from family farms and early-stage startups to Fortune 100s. Named customers include Shopify, Notion, Webflow, Poshmark, Eventbrite, Perplexity, CBRE, Anduril, Figma and Boys & Girls Clubs of America 

Scale (verified): Crossed $1B annualised revenue as of 1 June 2026 with positive free cash flow; ~$200B annualised purchase volume; 100%+ YoY enterprise growth with 3,200+ customers at $100k+ annualised revenue 

Business model: Free base tier, Ramp Plus at $15/user/month, custom enterprise pricing; interchange and payment fees drive economics 

Leadership: Co-founders Eric Glyman and Karim Atiyeh now serve as Co-CEOs; the company also named a new CTO and head of engineering, framing AI as central to strategy 

. Will Petrie was promoted to CFO and Geoff Charles to CPO 

. Max Freeman is SVP of Sales (per his LinkedIn profile, elevated from VP in June 2026 — profile-sourced, worth verifying) 

Competitive set: Brex, BILL Spend & Expense, Airwallex, legacy Amex/Capital One commercial cards

Likely buyer personas (hypotheses for a GTM/sales-tech sale)
These are inferred from hiring activity and public structure, not confirmed buying intent.
SVP/VP Sales (economic buyer) — Max Freeman is named publicly 

. Priorities: sustaining 100%+ enterprise growth, moving upmarket, standing up a UK team. Hypothesis: open to tools that improve rep productivity without adding headcount cost.
SDR/Sales Development leadership (strongest entry point) — they're hiring managers across SMB, Mid-Market and Enterprise segments simultaneously 

. Priorities: pipeline coverage, ramp time for new SDRs, outbound efficiency. Best fit for prospecting data, AI SDR, dialers, coaching and enablement.
RevOps / GTM Systems — implied by the scale of the sales org rather than directly evidenced. Priorities: CRM hygiene, territory and routing, attribution across a 70k-customer base and three segments. Usually the technical evaluator and the one who kills or approves tooling.
Product Marketing / Demand Gen leadership — the Lead PMM, Verticals role explicitly exists to turn industry differences into a growth advantage 

. Priorities: vertical messaging, ICP definition, UK market entry. Good fit for intent data, ABM and content/campaign tooling.
VP International / Expansion — Jacob Wallenberg, VP International Expansion, is the named public voice on the UK launch 

. Priorities: building UK pipeline from zero, local data coverage and compliance. Fit for EU/UK data providers and localisation.
Sequencing suggestion: start with the SDR managers and RevOps (feel the pain daily, faster to a meeting), and reference the SVP Sales as the exec sponsor rather than opening cold at that level.
